Suggested tab order for the story
  1. Gmail · HR Onboarding: read the offer and open the three attachments.
  2. Workday · HR Operations: from Home, open the Hire Employee inbox item (or the Hire app, or search “Hire Employee”). The form defaults from the requisition and shows Jessica’s personal information, including her SSN. Fix the location to Hybrid, then submit. Note the generated Employee ID.
  3. ADP: Process › Hire employee, three steps. Enter the SSN from Workday and the Workday Employee ID.
  4. ServiceNow: Home › Request something › New Hire IT Bundle, three steps. Enter the Workday Employee ID.
  5. Workday · HR Operations: Onboarding checklist, then Learning (courses and buddy). Then Teams.
  6. Gmail · HR Onboarding: send the welcome email to jessica.reyes@gmail.com. It appears in Gmail · Jessica.
  7. Gmail · HR Onboarding: email the onboarding summary to angella.smith@nodeway.com. It appears in Gmail · Angella.

Apps update each other when they’re open in the same browser: an email sent from one Gmail tab arrives in the other, and finishing the hire and onboarding in Workday · HR Operations fills in Jessica’s and Angella’s Workday. To start over, double-click the bottom-left corner of any app or press Ctrl+Shift+Alt+R. That resets every open tab. Dates follow the computer’s clock.
