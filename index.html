Suggested tab order for the story
  1. Gmail · HR Onboarding: read the offer and open the three attachments.
  2. Workday · HR Operations: from Home, open the Hire app (or search “Hire Employee”), then hire Jessica. The hire completes straight away.
  3. ADP: Process › Hire employee, three steps.
  4. ServiceNow: Home › Request something › New Hire IT Bundle, three steps.
  5. Workday · HR Operations: Onboarding checklist, then Learning (courses and buddy). Then Teams.
  6. Workday · Angella: Refresh on Home to receive the audit trail.
  7. Gmail · HR Onboarding: send the welcome email. Gmail · Jessica: Refresh to receive it.

Each app has a small “Reset demo” link at the bottom to start over. Demo data is fictional.
